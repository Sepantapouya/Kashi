FONTLOG for Kashi
=================

This file provides detailed information on the Kashi font software.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Kashi (کاشی) is the Persian and Latin user-interface typeface of the Kashi
design system (https://kashi.sepantapouya.com). It is made for product
interfaces: buttons, forms, tables, dashboards and dense Persian text on
screens.

- Scripts: Arabic script (Persian, Arabic, Urdu basic coverage), Latin
- Weights: 9 (Thin 100 to Black 900) in two widths, plus a variable font
  with two axes: wght 100-900 and wdth 100-125 (Normal to Expanded)
- Builds:
    Kashi        full Persian + Latin (static: Kashi and Kashi Expanded)
    Kashi FaNum  Latin digits 0-9 display as Persian digits ۰-۹
    Kashi NoEn   Latin letters removed, so English text uses a fallback font
- Formats: TTF (static + variable), WOFF2
- OpenType: init/medi/fina, rlig, calt, mark/mkmk, kern, tnum, frac,
  ss02 (toman and rial signs), ss03 (word signs), ss04 (round dots),
  ss05 (sharp square dots) and the rest of Dastkhat's features

Kashi is a derivative of Dastkhat by Dastyar (SIL OFL 1.1), which is a
derivative of Vazirmatn by Saber Rastikerdar (SIL OFL 1.1). The Latin glyphs
derive from Roboto (Apache License 2.0).


What Kashi changes
------------------

Kashi is built from the Dastkhat v1.220 sources by sources/derive.py. Every
step is a rule applied the same way in every master:

1. Geometric curves. In the Persian letters, each curve's off-curve points
   move 40% of the way toward the corner of its tangents, so bowls and loops
   become calmer and more geometric while keeping Vazirmatn's softness.
2. Low body, shallow descenders. Heights between the baseline stroke and the
   top of the bowls scale by 0.86, so bowls and teeth sit lower while the
   ascenders keep their height; the air inside bowls below the baseline
   scales by 0.86. The baseline, top and bottom stroke bands move without
   squashing, so every stroke keeps its thickness. Dots and anchors move with
   the letter.
3. Latin at Vazirmatn's size. Latin, punctuation and symbols scale up by 5%
   (Dastkhat had made them smaller), blended toward the lighter master so
   they keep their weight; kerning scales with them.
4. Width axis. The Expanded masters stretch every glyph by 20%; each glyph is
   first blended toward the neighbouring master so the vertical stems end up
   only 8% heavier than in the normal width.
5. Tile dots. Every square dot becomes a soft square tile, corners 18% of its
   side. ss04 swaps in round dots and ss05 sharp square dots, in every
   dotted glyph.
6. Tighter tabular digits. Dastkhat's tnum cell is far wider than its digits;
   Kashi's is the widest digit's ink plus a small margin, each digit centred.
7. New names: Kashi, Kashi FaNum, Kashi NoEn, and Kashi Expanded for the
   static expanded width.
